# Code of Conduct

QcY LiveLog Monitor welcomes constructive technical collaboration.

## Expected behaviour

Participants are expected to:

- communicate respectfully;
- discuss code and ideas rather than attacking people;
- accept technical disagreement without hostility;
- provide actionable feedback;
- respect privacy and security boundaries;
- avoid publishing another person's sensitive data.

## Unacceptable behaviour

The following is not acceptable:

- harassment or personal attacks;
- threats or intimidation;
- discriminatory or degrading language;
- deliberate disclosure of private information;
- repeated disruption of project discussions;
- knowingly submitting malicious code or credentials.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, hide or remove content and may restrict participation when behaviour materially harms the project or its contributors.

When reporting conduct problems, avoid public disclosure of private personal information.
